# Value Pod Matching Engine: Concierge Operating Instructions

Version: April 2026
For use by: Summer, Gabby, and any additional concierge staff


## Overview

Value Pods are small groups of 1 expert + up to 4 founders, matched by the founders' biggest current challenge. Your job is to make sure every founder lands in the right pod and every expert knows who they are working with before the session starts.


## Timeline and Phases

### Phase 1: Pre-Event Setup (1 to 2 weeks before)

**Fill in the Expert Roster.**

1. Open the CSV file (Section 1: Expert Roster).
2. For each confirmed expert, enter their name, expertise category, and up to three specific strengths. Example: an expert in "Sales system buildout" might have strengths listed as "B2B enterprise sales," "sales team hiring," and "CRM implementation."
3. Set Availability to Y.
4. Set Max Pods Per Event. Default is 2. Some experts prefer 1. Confirm with each expert directly.
5. Add any relevant notes. Examples: "Prefers to work with SaaS founders," "Arriving late, available for second session only."

**Collect Founder Intake information.**

Option A (preferred): Send a short intake form with the RSVP confirmation. The form should ask:
- Full name
- Company name
- Current annual revenue range (use the ranges from the CSV: $2M-$5M, $5M-$10M, $10M-$25M, $25M-$50M, $50M-$100M, $100M-$200M, $200M+)
- Top challenge (provide the 12 category options as a dropdown or list)
- Secondary challenge (same list)

Option B (backup): Collect this information at check-in. Print a short card with the same fields. Have the founder fill it out while you prepare their lanyard.

Enter all collected data into Section 2 of the CSV before guests arrive.


### Phase 2: Matching (day of event, before doors open)

**Run the matching logic in this order:**

Step 1: Group founders by Top Challenge. Sort the Founder Intake rows so founders with the same Top Challenge are grouped together.

Step 2: Assign each group to an available expert whose Expertise Category matches the Top Challenge.

Step 3: Check pod size. Each pod holds a maximum of 4 founders. If a challenge group has more than 4 founders, split it into multiple pods. You will need a second expert in the same category, or assign the overflow founders to a pod using their Secondary Challenge instead.

Step 4: Handle small pods. If a challenge group has only 1 founder, check their Secondary Challenge. If another pod matches that Secondary Challenge and has room (fewer than 4 founders), move the solo founder there. Only do this if the Secondary Challenge is a genuine fit. When in doubt, keep the solo founder in a 1-on-1 pod with the expert.

Step 5: Fill in Section 3 (Pod Assignments). For each pod, record the pod number, the assigned expert, each founder's name (up to 4), the challenge theme, the room or location, and the time slot.

Step 6: Update Section 2. For each founder, fill in the Matched Expert and Pod Number columns.

**Tiebreaker and balancing rules:**

- When two pods in the same category are uneven, use Secondary Challenge to decide which pod a founder fits better.
- When a founder's Top Challenge has no available expert, check their Secondary Challenge for a match. If neither challenge has an available expert, flag the founder for the host (see Escalation section below).
- Revenue range does not determine pod assignment, but avoid placing a $2M founder in a pod themed around $50M+ scaling unless no other option exists.


### Phase 3: Arrival and Check-In

**When a founder arrives:**

1. Find their name in Section 2 of the CSV.
2. Update their Arrival Status to "Arrived."
3. Hand them the correct lanyard:
   - Yellow lanyard = VIP member
   - Purple lanyard = Founders Lounge member
   - Check their membership tier before handing out a lanyard. If unsure, confirm with the host.
4. Tell them their pod number and location. Example: "You are in Pod 3. That is in the back lounge, starting at 7:15."
5. If there is any personalization note in the Concierge Notes column, act on it. Example: if a note says "Wants to meet [specific expert]," confirm that connection is set up or flag it for the host.

**When an expert arrives:**

1. Find their name in Section 1 of the CSV.
2. Hand them a brief sheet (or show them on a device) with:
   - Their pod number(s)
   - The names and companies of the founders in their pod(s)
   - The challenge theme
   - Any relevant notes from the Concierge Notes column on each founder's row
3. Walk them to their pod location.
4. If any founders in their pod have not yet arrived, let the expert know who is still expected.


### Phase 4: During the Event

- Track Arrival Status in real time. If a founder is a no-show 15 minutes after the pod session starts, note it and inform the expert.
- If a founder arrives late, walk them to their pod and quietly introduce them to the expert.
- If a founder finishes early or wants a second session, check if any other expert has capacity (compare pods assigned vs. Max Pods Per Event). Coordinate with the host before reassigning.


## Escalation Protocol

**Use this when a founder's challenge does not match any available expert.**

1. Check the founder's Secondary Challenge. If a match exists, assign them there.
2. If no match exists for either challenge, write "UNMATCHED" in the Matched Expert column and highlight the row.
3. Notify the host immediately. The host will either:
   - Handle the founder personally during the pod session
   - Pair the founder with the closest-fit expert and brief the expert on the mismatch
   - Seat the founder at the host's table for a direct conversation
4. Do not leave an unmatched founder waiting without acknowledgment. Tell them: "We are setting up a special session for you. Give us just a few minutes."


## Quick Reference: Expertise Categories

Use these exact strings when filling in Top Challenge and Secondary Challenge fields. Do not abbreviate or rephrase them.

1. Scaling operations ($2M to $10M)
2. Scaling operations ($10M to $50M)
3. Scaling operations ($50M+)
4. Fundraising / capital strategy
5. Exit planning / M&A
6. Sales system buildout
7. Marketing / brand positioning
8. Hiring / team building
9. Legal / IP / contracts
10. Tech / product development
11. Mindset / founder burnout
12. Financial modeling / CFO-level strategy


## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Do not assign more than 4 founders to a single pod. Split and create a second pod instead.
- Do not assign an expert to more pods than their Max Pods Per Event allows.
- Do not guess lanyard color. Confirm membership tier before handing out lanyards.
- Do not skip the expert briefing. Every expert should know their founders' names and challenges before the session starts.
- Do not leave the Arrival Status column blank. Mark every founder as "Not Arrived," "Arrived," or "No-Show."


## Supplies Checklist

Bring to every event:
- Printed copy of the full CSV (all three sections) on paper as backup
- Yellow lanyards (VIP)
- Purple lanyards (Founders Lounge)
- Blank intake cards (for founders who did not complete the pre-event form)
- Pens
- A device with the CSV file open for real-time edits
- Pod location signs or table numbers
